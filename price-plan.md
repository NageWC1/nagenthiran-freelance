# Price Plan — Friend Network vs. Local Business Premium

Started 2026-09-04, prompted by a conversation about a friend charging AUD $5,000
for a "simple static site with SEO support" to Australian clients, and whether
that kind of pricing is reachable in the UK. This file is exploratory — nothing
here is committed or launched. Companion to `freelance-monetization.md` (the
live pricing/positioning for the track that's actually running) and
`freelance-operations.md` (delivery mechanics for that track).

## 1. Market pricing — where the $5,000 AUD claim actually sits

Currency wasn't confirmed with the friend — treated as AUD here since he's
quoting Australian clients (the far more common pairing for a resident
freelancer). AUD $5,000 ≈ £2,600 at current rates; if it was USD instead,
≈ £3,900.

| Market | Simple static site (one-off) | SEO — one-time foundation | SEO — ongoing/monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Australia** (friend's market) | AUD $3,000–$7,000 | $150–300/page, often bundled in | Not usually included in the build price |
| **UK — freelancer** | £800–£3,000 (most straightforward builds £1,200–£2,000) | Rarely itemized separately at this tier | — |
| **UK — "professional quality" budget** (what small businesses are told to expect) | £2,000–£5,000 | Usually bundled | — |
| **UK — agency-tier** | £2,400–£8,000 (≈$3,000–$10,000) | Bundled | — |
| **UK — local SEO as its own service** | — | — | £300–£1,500/mo, most small businesses land at £500–£1,000/mo |

**Verdict:** $5,000 AUD (≈£2,600) is roughly mid-market for Australia, not an
outlier or a special skill the friend has — and it lands inside the UK's own
"professional quality" range too. The number itself is achievable in the UK
market. The harder question is everything in section 2.

## 2. The real decision — two different businesses, not one price change

| | **Track A — Friend Network** (built, launching) | **Track B — Local Business Premium** (the $2–3k tier) |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | £150–350 per site | £2,000–3,000 per site + optional £500–1,000/mo SEO retainer |
| **Client** | Friends, students, personal contacts | Real small businesses with revenue at stake — tradespeople, salons, restaurants, local services |
| **Why they pay** | Trust/relationship, cheap favour | Direct ROI — leads, calls, bookings |
| **Channel** | WhatsApp into UK friend groups | Chambers of commerce, BNI-style networking, local Facebook business groups, referrals, targeted cold outreach |
| **Pitch** | "I can build you a site" | "I get you leads" — site + SEO sold as one bundled outcome, not two separate line items |
| **Proof required** | None — warm relationship carries it | Case studies with real results — currently **zero**, nothing sent to a client yet |
| **Status** | Built, priced, flyers ready — 0 sent so far | Doesn't exist yet — no niche picked, no pitch built, no proof |
| **Risk if mixed** | — | A friend in the same network seeing a £250 quote next to a stranger's £2,500 quote is an awkward conversation — keep these visibly separate |

Bundling web + SEO from one provider is itself a real value prop worth using
in the Track B pitch, not just an upsell: a redesign done without SEO
oversight is a commonly cited cause of ranking drops, so "one person owns
both" solves a real client fear.

## 3. If Track B is pursued — staged pricing

| Stage | Price | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Proof-of-concept client(s)** | Near cost, or a steep discount | Trade for a real testimonial + permission to use as a case study — can't skip this with zero track record |
| **Standard offer, once 1–2 case studies exist** | £1,500–2,500 one-off (site + SEO foundation) | Matches UK market rate for the segment |
| **Recurring add-on** | £350–600/mo local SEO retainer | The actual long-term economic lever — a few retainer clients compounds far better than one-off fees |

## 4. What's missing before Track B is real

- A chosen niche (one type of local business, not "anyone with a shop")
- 1–2 case-study clients and honest before/after numbers (rankings, leads, bookings)
- An outreach motion built around those results, not a generic pitch
- A decision on whether this runs under the same `nagenthiran-freelance` /
  `nagenthiran-portfolio` identity, or stays visibly distinct from the
  friend-rate offer to avoid the pricing-clash risk in the table above
